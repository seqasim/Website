Core papers, textbooks, and introductory lectures for onboarding and journal club.

## Human intracranial EEG (iEEG) & single-unit recordings

Foundational methods and conceptual papers for working with neurosurgical recordings:

- Buzsáki, G., Anastassiou, C. A., & Koch, C. (2012). [The origin of extracellular fields and currents — EEG, ECoG, LFP and spikes](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn3241). *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, 13(6), 407–420.
- Einevoll, G. T., Kayser, C., Logothetis, N. K., & Panzeri, S. (2013). [Modelling and analysis of local field potentials for studying the function of cortical circuits](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn3599). *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, 14(11), 770–785.
- Mercier, M. R., Dubarry, A.-S., Tadel, F., Avanzini, P., Axmacher, N., Cellier, D., … Oostenveld, R. (2022). [Advances in human intracranial electroencephalography research, guidelines and good practices](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2022.119438). *NeuroImage*, 260, 119438.

## Recommended reviews & textbooks

Broad overviews and online textbooks for experimental design, coding practices, onboarding, and journal club:

- [Experimentology: An Open Science Approach to Experimental Psychology Methods](https://experimentology.io/) (Frank et al.) — start going through this experimental psychology textbook for study design, replication, measurement, and open science workflows.
- [The Good Research Code Handbook](https://goodresearch.dev/index.html) (Patrick Mineault) — start going through this coding resource for organizing, testing, and maintaining reliable research code.

## Educational videos: neural time series & EEG fundamentals

Introductory lecture videos (Mike X Cohen) on the biophysics and analysis of electrophysiological recordings:

1. [Origin of EEG](https://youtu.be/Bmt89hHyxuM?si=fwcztI_cjwQ4fbyD) — biophysical origin, significance, and interpretation of extracellular potentials
2. [Preprocessing basics](https://youtu.be/JMB9nZNGVyk?si=mg-VddKF-ZqNq6WH) — overview of standard preprocessing steps and artifacts
3. [Time-domain (ERP)](https://youtu.be/iFWrVzLYop0?si=jgSZk5UQguJGt9PR) — event-related potentials and time-domain averaging
4. [Rhythm-based analyses](https://youtu.be/3hk4z3yrMzk?si=stSIsMaZH9vW3Ipg) — motivations and methods for analyzing neural oscillations
5. [Interpreting time-frequency plots](https://youtu.be/s2MfmIx8wv4?si=7th8mEBbga4a0bE5) — reading and interpreting spectral power and phase across time and frequency
