Welcome to the Qasim Lab. Use this checklist during your first week. Ask Salman or a lab member if anything is unclear — we would rather over-explain than leave you stuck.

## Rutgers NetID and campus systems

1. Confirm your **Rutgers NetID** is active and that you can sign in to [myRutgers](https://my.rutgers.edu/).
2. Set up your Rutgers email (or verify forwarding) and add Salman (`salman.qasim@rutgers.edu`) plus relevant lab contacts.
3. Enroll in any required payroll / HR onboarding steps for your appointment type (student, postdoc, staff).

## Required trainings

Complete these early; some are required before you can work with human subjects data or clinical collaborators:

- **CITI Human Subjects** modules (Rutgers Human Subjects Protection Program)
- **HIPAA / privacy** training as directed by Rutgers and clinical partners
- Any **lab safety** or building-specific modules requested by RWJMS / BHI

Links to CITI and IRB portals are on the [Resources hub](/resources).

## Building and badge access

- Arrange **badge / building access** for RWJMS Neurosurgery and Brain Health Institute spaces with Salman and departmental admin.
- Note lab meeting rooms, clinical collaborator locations, and after-hours entry rules.
- Confirm where desks, shared computers, and secure data workstations live.

## Lab communication and tools

1. Join the **lab Slack** (invite from Salman) and introduce yourself.
2. Get on the **lab Google Calendar** for meetings, journal club, and shared deadlines.
3. Request access to the **lab GitHub** organization / repositories (`github.com/seqasim`).
4. Bookmark this wiki (`/resources`) and skim the other chapters before your first lab meeting.

## First-week milestones

- [ ] NetID, email, and calendars working
- [ ] CITI / HIPAA / safety trainings started or scheduled
- [ ] Badge access requested
- [ ] Slack + calendar + GitHub access
- [ ] Schedule a 1:1 with Salman to align on project goals and reading
- [ ] Skim [Foundational Literature](/resources/foundational-literature) and [Technical Skills](/resources/technical-skills)
